# TimeGrid v3.14.9 — stable historical memo selection

Status: Verified-local. User requested direct verification and repair of retrospective
memo application to a completed previous-day event. D-027 authorizes verified
implementation deployment. No new planning or completion semantics are introduced.

## Observations and causal boundary

The affected authenticated Chrome tab displayed v3.14.8 and a 2026-09-05 event
editor. Its event had minute-resolution bounds without exact timestamps or
continuity lineage. The existing candidate lookup returned the previous day's
routine. A trusted pointer selection at the candidate's actual rendered location
applied the routine link; saving preserved the event bounds and reopening after
sync retained the selected memo. A date lookup or metadata-write failure was not
reproduced on that record; it would be incorrect to classify either from the
user's report alone.

A separate interaction failure was observed: focusing the memo while the native
dialog was entering initially positioned its candidate beside the input, but
after the dialog's transform disappeared the candidate moved hundreds of pixels.
A pointer at its earlier location dismissed the modal instead of selecting the
candidate. The shared placement function compensated a transformed ancestor's
fixed-position coordinate origin only once. Completion of that transform changed
the containing block without a resize/scroll event to trigger another placement.
The list's own entry transform also participates in the measured offset.

The late-focus trusted-input regression exposed the upstream cause. Instrumentation
recorded one showModal call and one open mutation. The entrance finished and no
animation remained; at candidate pointerdown its position was correct. Three
milliseconds later, pointerup observed dlgIn restarted at time zero, restoring
scale .96/translateY(16px) without another open/class mutation. The candidate moved
from y623 to y848; the subsequent click hit the footer and the outside-click
handler hid the list. Persistent `dialog[open]` animation eligibility let a later
interaction restyle restart entrance motion. Focus-preservation experiments did
not repair this layout restart and are absent from the final source.

## Repair and preserved contracts

All seven application modal openings now use showDialog, which owns a one-shot
dialog-entering class. Its own entrance completion removes that class and
reconciles open popup positions; close clears it, and closing/bdout excludes entry
motion. A later touch/focus restyle cannot re-arm the entrance. The shared popup
placement also coalesces follow-up frames only while finite list/ancestor
animations run and recomputes after they settle. Hidden/disconnected lists stop;
no idle polling is added. Note/activity autocomplete and area pickers reuse the
same placement. Original click selection, native touch behavior, keyboard handling,
and the entrance/exit durations remain unchanged; no pointerup selection shortcut.

Historical candidate ownership and explicit stable Todo/Routine IDs remain under
the v3.14.6 contract. Free notes remain mutually exclusive with selected links.
C-TRACK-01/02, C-SYNC-01/02 and C-CATALOG-01 remain: no time rewrite, completion,
schema, Firestore rule, storage migration or Automation runtime change.
